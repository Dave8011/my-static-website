Neuro Rehabilitation in Mumbai

Neuro-rehabilitation focuses on the brain and the central nervous system. The primary goal is to stimulate neuroplasticity—the brain's ability to form new synaptic connections. This allows healthy parts of the brain to take over functions previously managed by damaged areas. It is a process that requires high repetition, intensity, and a multidisciplinary approach.

Comprehensive Care

We provide a 360-degree evaluation and a collaborative treatment process tailored to conditions such as Stroke, Parkinson's, Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI), and Spinal Cord Injury (SCI).

Our Integrated Multidisciplinary Approach

  • Physiotherapy: Gait training, balance retraining, and muscle awakening.
  • Occupational Therapy: Activities of Daily Living (ADL) training and cognitive rehab.
  • Speech Therapy: Addressing aphasia and dysphagia for safe communication and nutrition.
  • Robotic Rehabilitation: High-repetition, precise movements assisted by advanced technology.
