Stroke Rehabilitation in Mumbai

A stroke can drastically alter a person's life in an instant, leading to paralysis, speech impairments, and cognitive challenges. At The Rehab House, we provide the best stroke rehab in Mumbai through a coordinated, intensive treatment protocol designed to maximize recovery during the crucial neuroplastic window.

Targeted Paralysis Treatment

  • Early Mobilization: Initiating therapy as soon as medically stable to prevent complications.
  • Robotic Assistance: Utilizing cutting-edge robotic devices to perform high-repetition, precise movements that stimulate neural rewiring.
  • Aphasia & Dysphagia Care: Dedicated speech-language pathology to regain swallowing safety and communicative ability.
  • Functional Training: Real-world simulations so patients can relearn how to dress, eat, and return to an independent lifestyle.

Why Intensive Inpatient Rehab?

Outpatient therapy is excellent for maintenance, but the initial phase of stroke recovery requires medical stability and high intensity. Our inpatient admission facility provides 24/7 medical supervision and allows for an immersive environment where every activity is therapeutic.
